About the Project

This project, that is currently a work in progess, aims to think about Latina/o/é Literature through markers of space and place. By mapping texts and excerpts by their location of setting it demonstrates not only the long and current presence of Latina/o/é people but the ways in which stories are also shaped and marked by place. The hope is that it can serve as a digital tool within the classroom. This is also, in a small way, an honoring of the many hours I spent with my advisors inside and outside of class, talking through these books and having fun.

One of my advisors once suggested that a lot of my thinking works through my side-quests. We argue plenty if they count as creative practices or not. And she might actually kill me for quoting this, but it has stayed with me and has been, along with her, my constant source of inspiration: "the act of creating unlocks some of the analysis that feels more challenging." This project and my commitment to adding to it as often as I can is a reflection of my luck to be the student of wonderful human beings.

Mechanics of the Website

The tabs function as follows: There is a GIS map of Latiné literature, created using more of an approximation of narrative space as opposed to exact locations. Following that is the Textual Mapping, all locations represented on the map are layed by state location. Next to that I have included more general teaching materials as an added appendum to the mapping of Latiné literature. Finally the bibliography of all the texts included throughout this website.
