Narrative GIS Mapping

GIS Mapping Taught Through the Theory of Accompaniment

These are the materials I created originally for a workshop in the hopes of using it in my classroom. The materials aim at teaching Latin American migration through GIS mapping transformed into a 3D printed map of Mexico with different migrant experiences plotted. The experiences are pulled from a range of literary texts. The visual is centered in an attempt to help students see migration in a more material manner and make connections from lectures to the literary to the teaching materials.
