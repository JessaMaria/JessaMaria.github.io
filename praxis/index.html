Curating a Collection

About the Project

This project aims to think about different collections and how they shift through the visual. The visual here will be manipulated in different forms, to determine how information and knowledge is curated and in the process of curation understood.

Mini Collections

There are going to be a series of different collections. The first is through mapping images using ARC GIS. The second takes a literal form of visual and breaks 25 photos into smaller groups.
